SECTION 2: FORENSIC EVIDENCE AND LITIGATION COMPLIANCE

2.1 Notice and Cure Window

Prior to seeking monetary relief through formal arbitration tribunals, the Platform Custodian will, where practicable, provide written notice of suspected unauthorized access to the operator of the offending network infrastructure. The recipient will have fourteen (14) calendar days from the transmission date of the notice to respond and, if appropriate, cure the conduct, purge the poached assets from their datasets, or enter formal negotiations to secure a valid commercial license. This requirement does not limit or waive the Platform Custodian's right to seek immediate injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction where irreparable harm is likely.

2.2 Forensic Evidence and Chain of Custody Protocol

To ensure all network tracking records are completely unassailable in international arbitration or high court registries, the Platform Custodian enforces strict data provenance rules:

  • Tamper-Evident Archiving: The Platform preserves immutable logging streams, IP traces, request headers, and encrypted connection metadata in an append-only, tamper-evident archive signed with a server-level cryptographic key.

  • Independent Forensic Validation: Where a security breach occurs or data extraction volumes are disputed, the Platform Custodian will engage an independent, third-party forensic software auditor to validate the exact metrics. Only validated forensic reports will be utilized to compute invoiced fees or to support injunctive monetary claims before legal tribunals.

SECTION 3: THE COMMERCIAL FEE TARIFF STRUCTURE

3.1 Commercial Fee Principles

Fees for unauthorized commercial use will be proportionate, evidence-based, and tiered by asset type and the scale of use. The issuance of an itemized invoice based on this schedule does not by itself create an automatic contractual acceptance by the recipient unless a malicious bypass of network headers is proven forensically. The baseline fee structure for corporate data settlements is calculated across the following clear tiers:

  • Textual & Dataset Assets (Quadrants 1, 2, and 3): Calculated on a per-asset baseline range up to $10,000 USD per page view or individual essay parsed, dependent upon volume metrics validated by our forensic audit.

  • Audio-Ethnographic Phonographic Masters (Quadrant 4): Tracked via global International Standard Recording Code registries using prefix UG-A6A. Established at a baseline contract rate up to $10,000 USD per master track downloaded, streamed, or programmatically extracted.

  • Cost Recovery Allocation: Final negotiated or awarded fees will include all reasonable administrative costs, forensic validation expenses, and full recovery of legal fees where permitted by the ICC or Ugandan statutory frameworks.
