**\
PRIVACY POLICY AND DATA PROTECTION PROTOCOLS**

**INTRODUCTION**

Your presence within this Sovereign Repository is honoured. This Privacy
Policy sets forth the custodial principles that govern how your personal
contact data and transactional metadata are protected within this
digital sanctuary. It reflects our commitment to a strict zero trust
architecture, where privacy is secured not by corporate promises but by
deliberate design, minimal data collection, and decentralized static
infrastructure.

These protocols ensure that your interactions — whether scholarly,
musical, advisory, or patronage based — remain shielded from commercial
surveillance, behavioural profiling, and unauthorized extraction. Every
reader, supporter, and technical collaborator is invited to understand
these boundaries, for they uphold the dignity of your digital footprint
and preserve the integrity of this sovereign space dedicated to Story,
Memory, and African knowledge restoration.

**PREAMBLE: DATA SOVEREIGNTY AND CUSTODIAL OBLIGATION**

This Privacy Policy and Data Protection Protocol establishes the
sovereign governance framework under which all personal data is
processed on nicksonogwal.org. It is grounded in the statutory
requirements of the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (Act No. 2 of
2019) of Uganda, and aligned with international data protection
principles. \[2\]

The Platform operates on a model of Security Hardening by Complete
Absence, collecting only the minimal information required to deliver
digital products, verify transactions, and maintain operational
integrity. By accessing or interacting with this Platform, the User
acknowledges these custodial protocols and agrees to the lawful,
transparent, and strictly limited processing of their data as detailed
herein.

**SECTION 1: DATA CATEGORIES, GOVERNANCE PROTOCOLS, AND PROCESSING
PRINCIPLES**

**1.1 Structural Declaration of Complete Data Inversion**

This Privacy Policy defines the strict algorithmic boundaries, storage
protocols, and legal bases under which user data is processed on
nicksonogwal.org. Unlike standard corporate web spaces that treat user
behavior as an asset for commercial data-mining, this Platform operates
on a model of **Security Hardening by Complete Absence**. Because this
website uses a completely decoupled **Static Architecture**, it does not
host a server-side relational database to trace your movements, compile
behavioral profiles, or store your passwords. Your privacy is protected
primarily through infrastructure design rather than passive legal
promises. \[3\]

**1.2 Categories of Personal Data Collected**

The platform limits data collection strictly to information you provide
voluntarily during direct interactions. The system processes three
specific streams of information:

- **Identity and Contact Data:** This includes your full name, email
  address, nationality, and telephone number. This information is
  collected only when you voluntarily submit it through our secure
  subscription webhooks, the "Buy Me a Coffee" patronage portal, or when
  requesting a specialized rights clearance license. \[4\]

- **Decoupled Transactional Metadata:** When purchasing books or audio
  downloads, the financial systems collect tokenized payment
  confirmations, settlement reference strings, currency indicators, and
  country codes.

- **Anonymized Edge Network Metadata:** When your browser requests
  static files from our repository, the system processes technical
  diagnostic information. This includes encrypted IP addresses, browser
  agent headers, device configurations, and referral link data.

**Absolute Storage Exclusions:**

To maintain an ironclad, zero-trust financial environment, this Platform
**never sees, captures, parses, processes, or stores** your raw
financial credentials, credit/debit card numbers, CVV security codes, or
mobile money personal identification numbers (PINs). All payment steps
are handled off-site by our certified payment gateway partners in
accordance with **PCI-DSS Level 1 Cryptographic Tokenization
Frameworks**.

**1.3 Statutory Legal Bases for Data Processing**

In strict compliance with Section 13 of the **Data Protection and
Privacy Act, 2019 (Act No. 2 of 2019)** of Uganda \[4\] and Article 6 of
the international **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, your
information is processed only under the following clear legal criteria:

- **Explicit Contractual Performance:** Processing your name, email, and
  transactional metadata is legally required to deliver digital products
  or to execute single-user licensing terms directly to your inbox upon
  purchase.

- **Voluntary Informed Consent:** When you sign up for our global
  patronage feed, subscription tiers, or community newsletters, you
  grant the Platform Custodian explicit permission to send communication
  directly to your verified email address. This consent can be withdrawn
  instantly using the single-click removal links included in every
  update.

- **Legitimate Security Hardening Interventions:** Processing anonymized
  edge network metadata and masked IP records is an absolute structural
  necessity to maintain account security. It helps detect distributed
  denial-of-service (DDoS) traffic spikes, mitigate hacking scripts, and
  block commercial AI scraping bots from extracting data at our
  Cloudflare edge perimeter.

**1.4 Data Retention and Purging Schedules**

The platform rejects the practice of hoarding user data indefinitely.
Your information is subject to strict operational lifecycles:

- **Subscription Contact Logs:** Kept active only for the explicit
  duration of your voluntary subscription or patron relationship. If you
  opt out of the newsletter feed, your contact records are completely
  purged from our communication queues within 30 days.

- **Statutory Financial Records:** Tokenized transaction references,
  invoice summaries, and payment confirmations are kept for a mandatory
  period of **seven (7) years**. This fulfills statutory tax auditing
  requirements and anti-money laundering (AML) laws enforced by the
  **Bank of Uganda** and our processing networks.

- **Anonymized Edge Performance Logs:** Network traffic data and edge
  firewall diagnostic records are automatically overwritten, masked, or
  erased within **ninety (90) days** of generation.

**SECTION 2: USER RIGHTS, DATA TRANSMISSION PERIMETERS, AND SECURITY
AUDITING**

**2.1 Explicit Global Rights of the Data Subject**

No matter your physical or geographical location, this Platform
recognizes and enforces your foundational data rights. In strict
compliance with the **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (Act No. 2
of 2019)** of Uganda \[4\] and international frameworks such as the
**General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, users can exercise the
following options directly via the compliance portal:

- **The Right to Absolute Access:** You may submit a formal request to
  legal@nicksonogwal.org to receive a machine-readable summary of all
  personal contact data or transactional metadata associated with your
  email address.

- **The Right to Immediate Rectification:** If your contact metadata,
  name, or country indicator changes or contains errors, you can update
  your information by messaging payments@nicksonogwal.org.

- **The Right to Complete Erasure ("The Right to be Forgotten"):** You
  may request that your personal contact logs be wiped from our systems.
  Upon validation, your details will be permanently purged from all
  marketing and reader directories within 30 days. *Statutory
  Carve-Out:* This does not apply to tokenized transaction receipts that
  the platform is legally mandated to keep for seven years under Bank of
  Uganda tax auditing rules.

- **The Right to Restriction of Processing:** You can choose to halt the
  processing of your data while keeping your account active, disabling
  direct marketing communication while maintaining your core reader
  profile.

**2.2 Verification and Response Mechanics**

To safely process any access, correction, or erasure requests, the
Platform Custodian enforces strict verification rules to prevent social
engineering or adversarial data fishing:

- **Proof of Identity:** We will ask for reasonable, proportional proof
  of identity. This includes a valid government-issued ID alongside an
  explicit cryptographic email verification token sent directly to the
  domain address on file.

- **Timelines for Action:** We will formally acknowledge receipt of your
  request within **five (5) business days**. Validated requests will be
  executed and completed within **thirty (30) calendar days**. If
  structural archival checks require additional processing time, we will
  provide a written explanation and an extended, definitive timeline.

**2.3 Decoupled Data Transmission Perimeters and Third-Party Sharing**

To prevent unauthorized data commercialization, the Platform enforces
strict boundaries around data transmission. Data sharing occurs
exclusively with decoupled processing partners under strict operational
conditions:

- **Financial Aggregator Networks:** Transaction metadata is transmitted
  using encrypted tokens to
  [**Flutterwave**](https://flutterwave.com/ug/terms) and **DPO Group**.
  This data sharing is required to process credit cards or route funds
  to your **MTN Uganda Mobile Money wallet**.

- **Sovereign Infrastructure Providers:** Static files, open
  repositories, and encrypted contact forms are mirrored securely across
  cloud platforms like **Codeberg** and **GitHub Pages** under strict
  data processing agreements (DPAs) that forbid using your records for
  internal product analysis.

- **Edge Defense Firewalls:** Network performance data and masked IP
  records pass through **Cloudflare** networks to intercept malicious
  hacking scripts, mitigate automated DDoS traffic spikes, and block
  predatory AI bots before they can access site assets.

**2.4 The 72-Hour Data Breach Notification Protocol \[5\]**

While the Platform's Static Architecture makes it exceptionally secure,
it maintains a transparent, high-urgency incident response protocol to
handle potential network emergencies:

- **Immediate Mitigation:** If an attacker compromises a hosting mirror
  or accesses our static repository workflows, the technical team will
  immediately decouple the affected asset paths and use Cloudflare's Web
  Application Firewall (WAF) to block the malicious IP addresses.

- **Statutory Notification Window:** In strict compliance with Section
  23 of the **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (Act No. 2 of
  2019)** of Uganda, if a breach exposes personal data or transactional
  metadata, the Platform Custodian will notify the **National Data
  Protection Office** and all affected users via email within
  **seventy-two (72) hours**.

- **Content of the Alert:** The alert will clearly state the nature of
  the breach, the specific data categories involved, the immediate
  containment steps implemented, and practical advice on how users can
  protect their personal communications.

**2.5 International Cross-Border Data Transmissions**

Because the platform relies on decentralized edge nodes to deliver fast,
un-hackable performance worldwide, data may pass across international
borders. When information travels between Uganda and international edge
nodes in Europe or North America, it is protected by strict legal
safeguards:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** All external cloud providers
  and edge nodes are bound by verified Standard Contractual Clauses.
  These contracts guarantee that your data receives the same level of
  legal protection abroad as it does under the **Data Protection and
  Privacy Act, 2019 (Act No. 2 of 2019)** of Uganda.
