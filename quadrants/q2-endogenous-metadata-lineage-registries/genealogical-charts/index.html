Genealogical Charts

Orientation

This Level Three category preserves structured genealogical charts that map lineage, ancestry, clan branches, and intergenerational relationships within Lango and related communities. It is the sovereign archive of family trees, ancestral diagrams, and lineage pathways that define identity, belonging, and historical continuity. This page serves as the entry point for all genealogical visualizations under Quadrant 2.

Mandate and Purpose

  1. To document genealogical charts that preserve ancestral and clan relationships.
  2. To provide structured lineage diagrams for cultural, historical, and academic reference.
  3. To safeguard genealogical knowledge from distortion, loss, or extractive use.
  4. To support cultural continuity through accurate lineage mapping.
  5. To maintain sovereign custodial control over sensitive ancestral information.

Scope of Materials

  1. Family trees and clan lineage diagrams.
  2. Intergenerational charts showing ancestry and descent.
  3. Visual genealogical maps previously drafted or published elsewhere.
  4. Structured lineage charts derived from oral histories or archival notes.
  5. Diagrams illustrating relationships between clans, sub-clans, and ancestral houses.

Access Level

This page is Restricted Cultural Access. Genealogical charts may contain sensitive ancestral information and are therefore shared selectively. Public access may be limited to high-level diagrams, while detailed charts remain protected under cultural custodial protocols and the Sovereign Repository Terms of Use v2.2.

Custodial Logic

Genealogical charts are custodially held under Quadrant 2 — Endogenous Metadata and Lineage Registries. Their preservation follows the principles of Story, Memory, and Sovereignty (SMS), ensuring that ancestral knowledge remains protected, dignified, and contextually grounded. Custodial authority remains with the author and the cultural lineage from which these genealogical structures originate.
