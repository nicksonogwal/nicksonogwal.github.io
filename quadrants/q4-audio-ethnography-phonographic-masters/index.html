Scope of Quadrant 4

The scope of Quadrant 4 reflects the full breadth of my artistic identity. It houses all phonographic masters, vocal captures, musical arrangements, and ISRC-registered assets under my custodial authority. These are not hobbyist recordings — they are sovereign audio documents shaped by lived cultural experience and professional studio practice.

Quadrant 4 contains the following pillars:

Studio Masters: Final mixed tracks, WAV masters, high-resolution audio, mastering versions, alternate masters, and archival studio outputs.

Vocal Captures: Raw vocal takes, harmonies, spoken word, chants, cultural vocal expressions, isolated stems, and rehearsal captures.

Musical Arrangements: Instrumental layers, harmony structures, rhythmic design, arrangement drafts, orchestration notes, and structural evolution of songs.

ISRC Catalogue (UG-A6A): Official ISRC-registered tracks, metadata sheets, registration codes, release versions, compliance documentation, and distribution-ready assets.

Music Videos & Visual Masters: Official music videos, lyric videos, visualizers, performance recordings, cultural video captures, and high-resolution masters.

Licensing and Permissions

Quadrant 4 is governed by strict phonographic and performance rights. My masters, my compositions, and my vocal captures are protected under international copyright frameworks. I retain absolute ownership of all underlying compositions and studio outputs.

All materials in Quadrant 4 are protected under:

  • Phonographic Copyright (℗)
  • Performance Rights (©)
  • International Standard Recording Code (ISRC) Global Registration (UG-A6A)
  • The Rome Convention for the Protection of Performers, Producers of Phonograms and Broadcasting Organisations (1961)
  • World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) Performances and Phonograms Treaty

Users may:

  • Stream low-resolution previews for personal, non-commercial education,
  • Purchase full-quality tracks for private playback only.

Users may not broadcast, redistribute, sample, sync, or commercialize any asset without formal licensing.
