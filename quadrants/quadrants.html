Quadrants - The Sovereign Knowledge Matrix

Cultural and Epistemic Orientation - A Structured Gateway into Indigenous Sciences, Heritage Memory, Advisory Work, and Ethnographic Audio

The Quadrants form the sovereign organisational backbone of this archive. They provide a clear, dignified, and culturally grounded structure for presenting the full breadth of work across Indigenous Sciences, heritage registries, advisory portfolios, and ethnographic audio. Each Quadrant is governed by its own statutory and custodial framework, ensuring that knowledge is held, protected, and shared with integrity.

This four-part matrix is designed to honour origin, safeguard memory, and guide readers responsibly through the archive. The matrix is organised into four distinct Quadrants, each governed by its own statutory and custodial framework.

Quadrant 1 - Indigenous Sciences and Oral Literature (The Open-Access Hub)

Scope: Open-access decolonial humanities texts, historical critiques, translations of oral poetry, and community-facing ethnographic documentation.

Licensing: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

User Obligations:

1. Attribution (BY): Users must cite the original URL on nicksonogwal.org and attribute authorship to Henry Nickson Ogwal (HnO).

2. Non-Commercial (NC): No commercial use without a separate written contract.

3. No Derivatives (ND): Modified versions may not be distributed publicly.

Statutory Backing: Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act, 2006 (Uganda) and the Berne Convention.

Quadrant 2 - Endogenous Metadata and Lineage Registries (The Defensive Vault)

Scope: Structured cultural databases, ancestral lineage mappings, royal Lango praise-song registries, genealogical charts, and historical meta-records.

Licensing: Absolute Sui Generis Database Rights combined with customary African protection protocols.

Territorial Notice: Protected under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (Uganda) and the EU Directive 96/9/EC on Database Protection. Any systematic extraction, scraping, or derivative compilation constitutes a breach of cross-border data protection rules.

User Obligations: Users are granted a personal, non-transferable viewing license. Unauthorized extraction triggers infrastructure-level IP dropping and legal claims under Section 5 and Section 8.

Quadrant 3 - Applied Strategy and Advisory Portfolios (The Commercial Engine)

Scope: Proprietary consulting models, organizational case studies, advisory white papers, policy frameworks, and full-length published books.

Licensing: Strict Traditional Copyright (All Rights Reserved).

User Obligations: Access requires digital transactions, subscriptions, or formal retainers. No duplication, screenshots, or transmission without prior written authorization.

Client Obligations: Advisory clients are bound by perpetual non-disclosure agreements embedded into their service contracts.

Statutory Backing: WIPO Copyright Treaty and the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

Quadrant 4 - Audio-Ethnography and Phonographic Masters (The Studio Node)

Scope: Master sound recordings, vocal captures, musical arrangements, and spoken-word digital media.

Licensing: Phonographic Copyright (@) and Performance Rights (@). All masters and compositions remain under the Platform Custodian.

ISRC Mandate: All audio assets are watermarked and registered globally under the Platform Custodian's official International Standard Recording Code (ISRC) registrant prefix: UG-A6A.

User Obligations:

1. Low-resolution previews may be streamed for personal, non-commercial education.

2. Purchased tracks grant private playback rights only.

3. Public broadcasting, radio play, background sync, sampling, or digital distribution requires a formal synchronization and mechanical license issued directly via payments@nicksonogwal.org.

Statutory Backing: Rome Convention and WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty.

Together, these Quadrants form a sovereign matrix that protects cultural memory, organizes knowledge responsibly, defines clear custodial boundaries, guides readers through structured pathways, ensures statutory compliance, and preserves the dignity of origin.
