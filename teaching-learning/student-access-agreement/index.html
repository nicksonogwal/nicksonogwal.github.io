Student Access Agreement

Terms of Engagement for Learners

Sovereign Orientation

This Level Two page defines the terms under which learners may access, engage with, and benefit from materials within the Teaching and Learning branch. It builds on the Ground Zero understanding that learning begins before birth, continues through all wal cycles, and is governed by lineage, rhythm, memory, and custodial authority. The Student Access Agreement protects the dignity of African epistemology and ensures responsible engagement with sovereign knowledge.

Mandate and Purpose

  1. To establish clear expectations for learners accessing Teaching and Learning materials.
  2. To protect custodial boundaries and uphold lineage-based authority.
  3. To ensure that learning is conducted with cultural sensitivity, dignity, and respect.
  4. To prevent extractive, harmful, or decontextualised use of sovereign knowledge.
  5. To guide learners through responsible, wal-based engagement with African epistemology.

Scope of the Section

  1. Access rules for all Teaching and Learning materials.
  2. Expectations for cultural sensitivity and custodial respect.
  3. Guidelines for referencing, citing, and using sovereign knowledge.
  4. Boundaries for protected, sensitive, or lineage-specific content.
  5. Responsibilities for learners across all wal cycles.

Licensing and Permission

This page is Open Access. However, access to certain protected or sensitive materials may require explicit permission from the custodial authority. All learners must comply with the Sovereign Repository Terms of Use v2.2 and respect territorial boundaries when engaging with lineage-based knowledge. No content may be extracted, republished, or commercialised without custodial consent.

User Obligations (Reader Responsibilities)

Learners must honour wal-based learning, uphold cultural dignity, and respect custodial authority. They must avoid extractive use, refrain from decontextualising knowledge, and recognise that learning is a generational responsibility. All learners must follow citation rules, respect protected content, and maintain the integrity of sovereign knowledge systems. Violations of custodial boundaries may result in restricted access.

Custodial Logic and Territorial Authority

The Student Access Agreement is custodially held under the Teaching and Learning branch. Its authority is grounded in Story, Memory, and Sovereignty, ensuring that knowledge remains protected, contextual, and aligned with wal-based learning. Custodial authority remains with the author and the cultural lineage from which these epistemologies originate. Territorial boundaries must be respected at all times.

Why It Matters

This agreement protects African learning from distortion, misuse, and extractive engagement. It ensures that learners approach sovereign knowledge with humility, respect, and cultural awareness. By upholding this agreement, learners strengthen lineage continuity, protect ancestral knowledge, and contribute to the preservation of sovereign epistemology for future generations.

SMS — Story. Memory. Sovereignty.

These three pillars guide the preservation, transmission, and protection of all knowledge within the Teaching and Learning branch. Story anchors identity, Memory sustains lineage, and Sovereignty protects cultural authority. Nothing comes after SMS in the content hierarchy.
